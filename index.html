Where the area of 40 dies (490 mm² of silicon) actually went

13%
25%
11%
44%
  • Logic cells 13% — gates and flip-flops doing the design's actual work
  • SRAM 6% — memory macros
  • Filler and tap cells 25% — inserted by the tools to keep rows continuous and wells tied
  • Other 11% — routing channels, analog and custom layout, power grid and empty space
  • Pad ring 44% — the 350 µm border of I/O cells and bond pads

Packaging and precheck

How the designs will be packaged, and whether they passed the design-rule checks before manufacture.

  • 24 of 40 designs selected chip-on-board packaging.
  • 39 of 40 designs passed the platform precheck they were accepted on (2026-07-04 to 2026-07-16), and 39 pass their latest re-run; FC01 has no finished precheck recorded.

Core area is each design's CORE_AREA, read from its own LibreLane config at the commit that was checked; where that could not be read, the project template's, 442 µm in from each edge of the die. Density inside the seal ring is over the whole die less its 26 µm seal ring: all the silicon a slot gives its designer, pad ring included. "Typical" and "best" multiply the core area by this run's median and best core densities, and "densest" by the densest 1 mm² any design on this run reached; SRAM and analog blocks take their area out of that. The theoretical maxima fill the core with nothing but that one cell of AS 7t-3.3V, the library most used on this run, edge to edge with no routing.

Data and method

Everything on this page is measured from the public layout; the dates come from the published schedules.

  • Logic cells are standard cell instances, excluding fillers, taps, antenna diodes and tie cells.
  • Transistors are places where gate polysilicon crosses diffusion, not counting dummy fill.
  • Core area is each design's CORE_AREA, read from its own LibreLane config at the commit that was checked; where that could not be read, the project template's, 442 µm in from each edge of the die. Density inside the seal ring is over the whole die less its 26 µm seal ring: all the silicon a slot gives its designer, pad ring included.
  • Bond pads are the pad openings in the pad ring, or in a second ring of pads inside it for chip-on-board bonding; probe pads are the others, next to the blocks they test. Letters and logos on the pad layer are not pads.
  • Theoretical maximum tiles one cell at its placement boundary with no routing.
  • Densest mm² is the best 1 mm square anywhere in each die, sliding in 0.1 mm steps and avoiding SRAM.
